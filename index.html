Have Questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before we get started together.

Call (330) 242-8998
Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — absolutely. We provide free, no-obligation on-site estimates for all project types. We'll schedule a visit at your convenience, assess your property in person, and provide a detailed written quote at no cost to you.

How far in advance should I schedule a project?

For smaller projects like mulching and cleanups, we can typically schedule within 1–2 weeks. For larger hardscape or landscape projects, especially during peak spring and summer season, we recommend booking 3–6 weeks in advance to secure your preferred dates.

Do you handle both design and installation?

Yes. We are a full-service company — from initial design consultation and planning through complete installation and final walkthrough. One team, one point of contact, one accountable company from start to finish.

Are your prices fixed or can they change?

We provide itemized written estimates before any work begins, and we stand behind those numbers. If unexpected site conditions arise that could affect scope or cost, we communicate immediately and get your approval before proceeding. No surprise invoices — ever.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We serve residential homeowners and commercial clients including office parks, retail properties, HOAs, and small business exteriors. Commercial projects receive the same attention to detail and quality standards as our residential work.

What's the fastest way to improve curb appeal?

For the fastest, most dramatic impact, we recommend our Seasonal Refresh or Curb Appeal Upgrade packages — fresh mulch, crisp bed edging, and a shrub trim can completely transform how a home presents from the street, often in a single day.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Envision Outdoor Solutions carries full general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage, and we're happy to provide proof of insurance on request. Protecting our clients, our crew, and the properties we work on is non-negotiable.
